# How to Create Your First Blog Post

## Step 1: Login to Admin Panel
1. Go to: `http://localhost:3000/admin`
2. Login with:
   - Email: `admin@admin.com`
   - Password: `123456`

## Step 2: Navigate to Blogs Section
1. Click on "Blogs" in the sidebar
2. Click "New Blog Post" button

## Step 3: Fill Basic Information
1. **Title**: Enter your blog title (e.g., "10 SEO Tips for 2026")
2. **Slug**: Auto-generated from title (you can edit it)
3. **Excerpt**: Short description (max 300 chars)
4. **Content**: Full blog content
5. **Category**: Select from dropdown (SEO, Social Media, etc.)
6. **Status**: Select "Published" (important!)
7. **Featured Image**: Click "Upload Featured Image" and select an image
8. **Author Name**: Your name
9. **Featured**: Check if you want to feature this blog

## Step 4: SEO Optimization
1. **Meta Title**: SEO title (max 60 chars)
2. **Meta Description**: SEO description (max 160 chars)
3. **Focus Keyword**: Main keyword for SEO
4. **SEO Keywords**: Add multiple keywords (click Add button)
5. **OG Image**: Optional - for social media sharing
6. **Canonical URL**: Optional

## Step 5: Add Tags
1. Type tag name
2. Press Enter or click "Add"
3. Add multiple tags for better categorization

## Step 6: Save
1. Click "Create Blog" button at bottom
2. Wait for success message
3. Blog will appear on blog page: `http://localhost:3000/blog`

## Important Notes:
- Status MUST be "Published" for blog to show on public page
- Featured Image is required
- Meta Title and Meta Description are required for SEO

## Sample Blog Data:

**Title**: How to Improve Your Website SEO in 2026

**Excerpt**: Discover the latest SEO strategies and techniques that will help your website rank higher in search engines and drive more organic traffic.

**Content**: 
Search Engine Optimization (SEO) continues to evolve, and staying ahead of the curve is crucial for online success. In this comprehensive guide, we'll explore the most effective SEO strategies for 2026.

1. Focus on User Experience
Google's algorithms increasingly prioritize websites that offer excellent user experiences. This means fast loading times, mobile responsiveness, and intuitive navigation.

2. Quality Content is King
Create valuable, in-depth content that answers your audience's questions. Long-form content (1500+ words) tends to perform better in search rankings.

3. Optimize for Voice Search
With the rise of voice assistants, optimize your content for conversational queries and long-tail keywords.

4. Build Quality Backlinks
Focus on earning backlinks from authoritative websites in your industry. Quality over quantity is the key.

5. Technical SEO Matters
Ensure your website has proper schema markup, XML sitemaps, and clean URL structures.
